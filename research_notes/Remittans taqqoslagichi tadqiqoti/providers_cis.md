# Money transfer providers: Russia→Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan→Uzbekistan (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-22. Only public pages were used. No logins, no live calculator quotes. Every price below comes from a published tariff or article, not from a live quote. The source date is given next to each one. Tool budget was limited, so several providers have gaps; these are listed explicitly.

## Corridor context and structural changes in 2026

### Takeaway
In 2025, remittances to Uzbekistan reached $18.9B. Card-to-card (P2P to Humo/Uzcard) transfers now make up almost half of that, next to classic money-transfer systems. The biggest change in 2026: on 23–24 July 2026 the EU's 21st sanctions package hit Zolotaya Korona's settlement centre. Many Korona routes closed, but Russia→Uzbekistan still worked as of late July 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 remittances to Uzbekistan were $18.9B (+27.2%). Of this, $9.9B (52%) came via international money-transfer systems, $8.6B (46%) via P2P transfers to bank cards, and $397M (2%) via bank transfers. The article says low commissions drove P2P growth. — [Gazeta.uz, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/07/24/koronapay-sanctions/)
- On 23 Jul 2026 the EU's 21st package listed RNKO "Платёжный центр" (Zolotaya Korona's operator and settlement centre) and Cifra Bank (which handled part of the international transfers). Korona then stopped transfers to Georgia, Armenia and others. Transfers to Kazakhstan failed. Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan and Turkey were still available when checked on 24 Jul. — [Gazeta.uz, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/07/24/koronapay-sanctions/)
- Asakabank (Uzbekistan) temporarily suspended sending and receiving Zolotaya Korona transfers, citing "technical work", with no timeline. No other Uzbek bank had announced a suspension as of 24 Jul. — [Gazeta.uz, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/07/24/koronapay-sanctions/)
- Parallax says six routes closed (Georgia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, Armenia, Moldova, Tajikistan) and four remained (Kyrgyzstan, Turkey, Uzbekistan, China). Kazakhstan later partly resumed, in RUB/KZT only, with USD/EUR blocked. — [Parallax, 29 Jul 2026](https://news.parallax.company/post/2026-07-29-785-zolotaya-korona-zakryla-perevody-v-gruziyu-armeniyu-moldovu-/) (via search summary; other outlets cite "14 countries", so sources conflict on the count)
- ForteBank (KZ) said Korona transfers were suspended indefinitely. — [Digital Business, 24 Jul 2026](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-24/zolotaya-korona-perestala-rabotat-v-kazahstane-chto-proizoshlo-posle-novih-sanktsiy-es/) (via search summary)
- KoronaPay Europe is giving up its EU licence. The Central Bank of Cyprus started revocation. — [The Moscow Times, 20 Aug 2026](https://ru.themoscowtimes.com/2026/08/20/krupneishaya-platezhnaya-sistema-perevodov-rossii-uidet-s-rinka-es-iz-za-sanktsii-a203952) (via search summary)
- Earlier disruptions: after US sanctions in July 2023, several Uzbek banks (People's Bank, Kapital Bank, Ipoteka Bank) suspended Unistream operations. — [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/central-asia-caucasus-banks-end-money-transfer-cooperation-with-sanctioned-russian-firm-unistream-285612); [Daryo, 25 Jul 2023](https://daryo.uz/en/2023/07/25/banks-halt-services-for-russian-money-transfer-system-unistream-in-uzbekistan/)
- Contact was suspended by Uzbek banks in Feb 2024 after the Bank of Russia revoked QIWI Bank's licence. QIWI owned Contact. — [Kun.uz, 21 Feb 2024](https://kun.uz/en/news/2024/02/21/banks-of-uzbekistan-report-suspension-of-contact-system); [Daryo, 22 Feb 2024](https://daryo.uz/en/2024/02/22/uzbek-banks-halt-contact-payment-system-amid-qiwi-bank-license-revocation/)
- MIR cards do not work in Uzbekistan. Transfers to Uzbek bank accounts and Humo/Uzcard cards from Russia still work. — [DTF guide, 2026](https://dtf.ru/howto/4842935-perevody-iz-rossii-v-uzbekistan) (secondary)

### Inferences
- A comparison tool must treat provider availability as volatile, especially Korona, Unistream and Contact. Routes can disappear overnight because of sanctions, so a "last verified" timestamp and an availability flag per corridor are needed.
- Russia→Uzbekistan Korona was still live as of late July 2026. I found no confirmation of its status in November 2026.

### Gaps
- The current (Nov 2026) status of Korona RU→UZ and Asakabank's resumption was not confirmed.
- Whether Contact resumed in Uzbekistan after 2024 (for example under a new owner) was not checked.
- Western Union and MoneyGram status in Russia: no 2026 source found in this session. My prior knowledge, not re-verified here, is that both suspended operations inside Russia in March 2022. The report writer should treat this as unverified.

## Russia→Uzbekistan: providers, fees, FX, payout, limits

### Takeaway
Russian banks now charge about 1% for transfers to Humo/Uzcard by card number: Sber 1% (min 30 ₽, max 3 000 ₽), VTB 1% (min 50 ₽), T-Bank free up to 20 000 ₽/month, then from 1%. Alfa's phone transfer is 1.95%. Fintechs like Avosend charge a flat 29 ₽. In every case the FX rate is the provider's own and is not published as a margin over the CBU rate, so the "0% fee" claims hide FX cost.

### Cited Findings
**Sberbank**
- Transfer by phone: free. To a card: 1% (min 30 ₽, max 3 000 ₽). Limits: phone 300 000 ₽/day; card 1 000 000 ₽/day. Speed: phone instant, card up to 3 days. — [rahmat.ru / migrantexam.ru, 2026 article](https://rahmat.ru/blog/yuridicheskaya_informaciya/perevody_v_banki_uzbekistana_v_2026_godu_tarify_limity_i_kak_vybrat_samyy_vygodnyy_sposob) (secondary, via search summary; not checked against sberbank.ru)
- A forum user compared rates: "В Сбере сейчас курс 128,39, а в Авосенд 136,83". No date was given; the unit is presumably UZS per RUB. — [forum.awd.ru](https://forum.awd.ru/viewtopic.php?t=411466&start=2500) (anecdotal)

**VTB**
- By phone: free. To a Uzcard/HUMO card: 1% (min 50 ₽), up to 350 000 ₽ per operation (per vc.ru). The official page says fees are "from 0.5%", with limits up to 1M ₽ per transfer and 5M ₽ per month. Sources conflict. — [VTB official](https://www.vtb.ru/personal/platezhi-i-perevody/perevody-uzbekistan/); [vc.ru antiswap 2026](https://vc.ru/id2243207/3047200-kak-perevesti-dengi-iz-rossii-v-uzbekistan) (via search summary)

**T-Bank (Tinkoff)**
- Transfers by phone or card number are free up to 20 000 ₽/month (50 000 ₽ with Pro, 100 000 ₽ with Premium). Above that the fee is "от 1%" of the excess, paid by the sender. Card-number transfers reach all banks in Uzbekistan. An intermediary-bank fee is also mentioned. — [T-Bank help: Uzbekistan](https://www.tbank.ru/bank/help/cross-border/directions/u/uz/); [T-Bank cross-border](https://www.tbank.ru/payments-and-transfers/cross-border/) (via search summary; direct fetch failed with a TLS error in this environment)
- Third-party guides cite limits of 1 000 000 ₽ per transfer and 5 000 000 ₽ per period. These are unverified. — [rahmat.ru](https://rahmat.ru/blog/obshchestvo/kak_perevesti_dengi_v_uzbekistan_iz_rossii_vygodno)

**Alfa-Bank**
- By phone: 1.95% (min 30 ₽), max 1 000 000 ₽, instant. — [rahmat.ru / migrantexam.ru 2026](https://rahmat.ru/blog/yuridicheskaya_informaciya/perevody_v_banki_uzbekistana_v_2026_godu_tarify_limity_i_kak_vybrat_samyy_vygodnyy_sposob) (secondary)

**Zolotaya Korona / KoronaPay**
- Payout: cash at pickup points across Uzbekistan, or credit to a Uzcard/Humo card. The sender needs a passport and the recipient's name; no bank details are required. Limit: up to $10 000 per transfer and up to 10 transfers per month. Speed: from 10 minutes. No fee was given. — [vc.ru, 22 May 2026 (promotional article)](https://vc.ru/money/2942633-perevody-v-uzbekistan-iz-rossii-zolotaya-korona-banki-i-uzcard)
- One aggregated tariff line: "Узбекистан | в рублях | от 1 | 1,5%". Uzbek receiving banks say the fee is typically 1–1.5%; Poytaxtbank says 0% if the receive currency differs from the send currency. — [Poytaxtbank](https://poytaxtbank.uz/en/private/money-transfer/zolotaya-korona/); [Turonbank](https://turonbank.uz/ru/private/money-transfer/zolotaya-korona/) (via search summary; dates unclear, possibly outdated)
- Public calculator: [koronapay.com/transfers/online/](https://koronapay.com/transfers/online/). There is an offline tariff calculator at koronapay.com/transfers/tariffs/offline/, referenced in a [bank tariff PDF](https://ofb.uz/assets/files/tarify-zolotaia-korona.pdf). I found no published API or partner program in this session.
- koronapay.com/robots.txt (fetched 2026-11-22) applies to all user-agents. It disallows URLs containing `*country*`, `*filters*`, tracking parameters (`utm`, `ref=`, `phone=`, etc.), and paths such as `/transfers/mob/`. It points to `/sitemap.xml`. — [koronapay.com/robots.txt](https://koronapay.com/robots.txt)

**Unistream**
- One undated source puts the fee at 1% to Uzbekistan. Another says currency transfers are fee-free, with the cost likely built into the FX rate. A third describes 0% via the app from Uzcard, up to 100 000 ₽ per operation. Avosend lists Unistream's own limit as at most 200 000 ₽ per transfer. Speed: from 10 minutes. One snapshot rate was 1 RUB = 100.1205 UZS (undated). — [brobank.ru 2026](https://brobank.ru/unistream-uzbekistan/); [Avosend help](https://avosend.com/help/moi-perevody/perevody-s-vydachey-nalichnymi/limity-na-perevody-cherez-yunistrim/); [SQB](https://sqb.uz/individuals/money-transfer/unistream/) (via search summary; conflicting and partly undated)
- Unistream has been under US sanctions since July 2023, and several Uzbek banks stopped working with it then. — [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-unistream-us-sanctions/32518470.html); [Daryo 2023](https://daryo.uz/en/2023/07/25/banks-halt-services-for-russian-money-transfer-system-unistream-in-uzbekistan/)

**Contact**
- Cash payout by passport, usually in 10–30 minutes. It costs more than a card transfer. No tariff was found. — [brobank.ru / search summary](https://brobank.ru/unistream-uzbekistan/)
- Suspended by Uzbek banks in Feb 2024 (see above). — [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2024/02/21/banks-of-uzbekistan-report-suspension-of-contact-system)

**Avosend (fintech, Russian-licensed)**
- RU→UZ Humo/Uzcard fee is 29 ₽ per transfer. The sender pays RUB and the recipient gets UZS. Limits: 16 499 988 UZS per day per recipient card; 164 999 999 UZS per month. Delivery within 3 days. Registration is required. — [Avosend Humo/Uzcard page](https://avosend.com/popolnenie-kart-humo-i-uzcard/) (fetched 2026-11-22; page undated)
- A search snippet from an older version showed "4999,00 UZS" and "49 Rub or 1%". The tariff appears to have changed over time. — [Avosend](https://avosend.com/popolnenie-kart-humo-i-uzcard/) (via search summary)
- Parallax listed Avosend and Unired as working alternatives after the Korona sanctions. — [Parallax, 29 Jul 2026](https://news.parallax.company/post/2026-07-29-785-zolotaya-korona-zakryla-perevody-v-gruziyu-armeniyu-moldovu-/)

**Other**
- Pulsend claims card transfers with no fee, limited to 100 000 ₽/day and per month. — [pulsend.app](https://pulsend.app/) (self-reported)
- Crypto route (RUB→USDT→UZS P2P): about 2–4% all-in, with an ambiguous regulatory status in Uzbekistan. — [vc.ru 22 May 2026](https://vc.ru/money/2942633-perevody-v-uzbekistan-iz-rossii-zolotaya-korona-banki-i-uzcard) (promotional)
- Transfers over 500 000 ₽ require proof of the source of funds, which can add 1–2 business days. — [vc.ru 22 May 2026](https://vc.ru/money/2942633-perevody-v-uzbekistan-iz-rossii-zolotaya-korona-banki-i-uzcard) (unsourced claim)
- FX: conversion is at the sender bank's or payment system's rate. One guide says a bank's internal rate can differ from the market rate by 2–4% on large amounts. — [rahmat.ru / migrantexam.ru 2026](https://rahmat.ru/blog/yuridicheskaya_informaciya/perevody_v_banki_uzbekistana_v_2026_godu_tarify_limity_i_kak_vybrat_samyy_vygodnyy_sposob) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Fees are now low and similar, around 1% or flat. The real differentiator is the FX rate, which no provider publishes as a margin over the CBU rate. A comparator that shows only fees would mislead. The "amount received in UZS" has to come from a live quote.
- Unlike bank apps, Avosend and Korona have public web calculators, so they are the most "publicly verifiable". Russian bank rates are visible only inside the logged-in app.
- Korona's robots.txt `*country*` disallow would match calculator query strings that include parameters like `sendingCountryId`. Automated polling of Korona's calculator is therefore against its robots.txt, even though the endpoint is reachable without login (an inference from the pattern; the endpoint itself was not called).

### Gaps
- No live quotes (UZS received for 10 000 / 50 000 ₽) were obtained. The calculators are JavaScript-driven, and I did not call them because of the robots and no-automation constraints.
- I found no public JSON endpoint documentation or partner/affiliate API for Korona, Unistream, Contact or Avosend.
- Russian Post (Почта России) transfers to Uzbekistan: not researched in this session.
- Ozon Bank, Gazprombank and Rosselkhozbank tariffs were not found. A vc.ru article only says these banks support the route.
- The ToS of Avosend, Unistream and the Russian banks on automated access were not checked.

## Kazakhstan→Uzbekistan: providers, fees, FX, payout, limits

### Takeaway
KZ→UZ is dominated by bank-app card-to-card transfers to Humo/Uzcard. Halyk charges 0 KZT. Kaspi charges 0.95% (min 200 KZT) as of Jan 2026. The recipient gets UZS at the bank's own rate, shown only in-app. Zolotaya Korona Kazakhstan was disrupted by the July 2026 sanctions.

### Cited Findings
**Kaspi.kz (Kaspi Gold)**
- Fee 0.95% of the amount, min 200 KZT. The amount is entered in KZT or UZS. The conversion rate is shown on the transfer page before confirmation. Path: Переводы → Международные переводы → 16-digit Humo/Uzcard number. Page updated 04.01.2026. — [Kaspi Guide](https://guide.kaspi.kz/client/ru/transfers/services/international/q17449) (fetched 2026-11-22)
- Launched in March 2025. It was free until 16 May 2025, then 200 KZT flat for up to 300 000 KZT (superseded). Limits in 2025 were up to 10 transfers and 1M KZT per day, and 4M KZT per month (not re-verified for 2026). The recipient's name is shown automatically. — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/kaspi-zapustil-perevody-v-uzbekistan); [bizmedia.kz, 26 Mar 2025](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-03-26-otkryty-perevody-v-uzbekistan-cherez-kaspi-v-sistemy-uzcard-i-humo/) (via search summary)

**Halyk Bank**
- App: 0 KZT regardless of amount. Branch: 0%. Payout to UzCard and HUMO only. The recipient is credited in UZS with automatic conversion, at an undisclosed rate. Instant. App limits: 100 transfers per day. With a trusted device: $2 500 per transaction, $5 000 per day, $150 000 per month. Branch: $10 000 per day, $50 000 per month. No calculator on the page. — [Halyk official](https://halykbank.kz/index.php/transfer/perevody-v-uzbekistan) (fetched 2026-11-22)
- A 2022 article cited 150 KZT (outdated). — [Gazeta.uz 2022 / search summary](https://tengrinews.kz/money/kak-otpravit-dengi-v-uzbekistan-s-minimalnoy-komissiey-471462/)
- Halyk changed its interbank transfer fees in Oct 2026. Whether this affects UZ transfers was not checked. — [Digital Business, 7 Oct 2026](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-10-07/kakie-teper-komissii-v-halyk-bank-pri-perevode-v-drugie-banki-i-chto-izmenitsya-cherez-51-den/)

**BCC (Bank CenterCredit)**
- 200 KZT per transaction, with promos at 0 KZT (a zero-fee period through 30.09.2025 was cited). Humo/Uzcard payout by phone or card. Instant. — [kaz-trud.ru, 18 Apr 2026](https://kaz-trud.ru/articles/kak-otpravit-dengi-v-uzbekistan-iz-kazahstana-rabochie-sposoby-komissii-i-riski)

**Paysend (KZ)**
- Fees from 199 KZT. Payout to Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, Uzcard and Humo. The rate is shown before confirmation. — [kaz-trud.ru, 18 Apr 2026](https://kaz-trud.ru/articles/kak-otpravit-dengi-v-uzbekistan-iz-kazahstana-rabochie-sposoby-komissii-i-riski)

**Kassa24 (terminals)**
- Humo/Uzcard top-up via terminals, max 200 000 KZT per operation. — [kaz-trud.ru, 18 Apr 2026](https://kaz-trud.ru/articles/kak-otpravit-dengi-v-uzbekistan-iz-kazahstana-rabochie-sposoby-komissii-i-riski)

**Zolotaya Korona Kazakhstan**
- Cash or card payout. Disrupted from 24 Jul 2026. ForteBank suspended it indefinitely. Parallax reports a partial resumption in RUB/KZT only. — [Digital Business](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-24/zolotaya-korona-perestala-rabotat-v-kazahstane-chto-proizoshlo-posle-novih-sanktsiy-es/); [Parallax](https://news.parallax.company/post/2026-07-29-785-zolotaya-korona-zakryla-perevody-v-gruziyu-armeniyu-moldovu-/)
- Freedom Bank KZ confirmed that transfers with Cifra Bank are unavailable. — [Gazeta.uz, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/07/24/koronapay-sanctions/)

**General FX caveat**
- The sender's service converts first. The recipient's bank may then convert a second time with its own fee. — [kaz-trud.ru, 18 Apr 2026](https://kaz-trud.ru/articles/kak-otpravit-dengi-v-uzbekistan-iz-kazahstana-rabochie-sposoby-komissii-i-riski)

### Inferences
- In KZ→UZ, as in RU→UZ, the zero-fee leaders (Halyk) make their money on FX, and none of the KZ banks publish their KZT→UZS rate publicly outside the app. Public verifiability is low. Only Paysend and Korona have public web calculators.

### Gaps
- Freedom Bank and Jusan (now renamed; status not checked) tariffs for UZ cards were not found. Western Union KZ→UZ pricing was not found (WU's KZ terms page exists: [westernunion.com/kz](https://www.westernunion.com/kz/ru/legal/terms-conditions.html)).
- No live KZT→UZS rates were observed for any provider.

## Receiving side in Uzbekistan and public verifiability / APIs

### Takeaway
Uzbek apps (Paynet, Payme, Click, Uzum, TBC UZ) are mainly receiving and top-up endpoints for Humo/Uzcard. Inbound transfers land on the card in UZS. I found no provider in either corridor that publishes a documented public pricing API. Verifiability ranges from public web calculators (Korona, Avosend, Paysend) to app-only pricing (all the big RU/KZ banks).

### Cited Findings
- Spot.uz publishes periodic reviews of card-to-card fees in Uzbek banks and payment apps (Aug 2025, Mar 2026). These cover domestic and outgoing fees for apps such as TBC UZ, Payme and Click. — [Spot.uz, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/03/03/money-transfers/); [Spot.uz, 25 Aug 2025](https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/08/25/card-to-card/) (not fetched; contents not verified)
- TBC UZ has a FAQ on app transfer fees. — [TBC UZ FAQ](https://tbcbank.uz/ru/faq/category/3/tbc-perevody/) (not fetched)
- Bank.uz lists Korona and Unistream pickup points in Uzbekistan and a card-to-card fee comparison. — [bank.uz Korona](https://bank.uz/money-transfer/zolotaya-korona); [bank.uz/perevodi](https://bank.uz/perevodi)
- Korona's robots.txt disallows `*country*`, `*filters*` and tracking parameters for all bots. — [koronapay.com/robots.txt](https://koronapay.com/robots.txt) (fetched 2026-11-22)

### Inferences
- A practical design for a remittance comparator: (a) take fees from published tariff pages (Kaspi Guide, Halyk, T-Bank help, Avosend), which are stable and crawlable, and (b) not scrape live FX quotes from Korona and similar sites without permission. Better to seek partner or affiliate access, or crowdsource "amount received" reports.

### Gaps
- Paynet, Payme, Click, Uzum and TBC UZ fees for receiving cross-border transfers were not verified in this session.
- I found no affiliate or partner programs for Korona, Avosend, Kaspi or Halyk.
- The ToS clauses on scraping for all providers were not reviewed.
